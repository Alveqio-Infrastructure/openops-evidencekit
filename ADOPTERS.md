# Adopters

OpenOps EvidenceKit is in early alpha. This page tracks privacy-safe adoption
signals, design partners, and public use cases.

Do not add customer names, private infrastructure details, private hostnames,
private IP addresses, screenshots with real data, or confidential operational
facts. Public adoption notes should describe the workflow, not the sensitive
environment.

## Add Your Organization

Open an adopter story issue or pull request with:

- organization name or anonymous description;
- team type such as MSP, nonprofit, association, platform team, agency, or
  self-hoster;
- which evidence domains you use;
- what changed in your readiness review process;
- whether the note may be listed publicly.

Anonymous entries are welcome when naming the organization would reveal too much
about internal operations.
