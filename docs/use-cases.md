# Use Cases

OpenOps EvidenceKit is for teams that need practical operations evidence before
they need a heavy compliance platform.

## Infrastructure Readiness Check

A consultant or MSP can collect backup, restore, monitoring, access, patch,
TLS, mail, software inventory, and runbook evidence before onboarding a client.
The generated report becomes a shared baseline for scope, risks, and next
actions.

## Managed Service Handoff

An operations team can bundle evidence, policy results, a risk register, action
tickets, and a review attestation before handing a system to another maintainer.
The bundle makes the current state reviewable without exposing raw secrets.

## Nonprofit And Association Operations

Volunteer-run organizations often rely on a few administrators and informal
knowledge. EvidenceKit can turn quarterly checks into repeatable Markdown
reports that show whether backups, monitoring, runbooks, and emergency contacts
are still usable.

## Self-Hosted Service Reviews

Self-hosters who run community services can use synthetic policy packs and local
collectors to prove that basic operational hygiene exists before publishing or
sharing a service.

## Small Platform Team Audits

Small SaaS or platform teams can run evidence checks in CI and publish machine
readable outputs such as JUnit, SARIF, Prometheus metrics, badges, and review
packs. This supports recurring readiness reviews without introducing a vendor
lock-in platform.

## Security And Recovery Drills

Teams can combine vulnerability scan output, SBOM evidence, patch status,
firewall posture, access exposure, restore proof, and incident readiness
reports into one operational view. The result is useful for internal reviews,
customer assurance, and remediation planning.

## Documentation Freshness

EvidenceKit can connect service catalog entries, runbooks, escalation contacts,
and generated reports. This makes stale documentation visible before it becomes
an incident response problem.
